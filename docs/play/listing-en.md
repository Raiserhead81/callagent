**App name (max 30):** Gemma Call Assistant  *(20)*

**Short description (max 80):** Unknown caller? Your AI assistant answers and sums up what they wanted.  *(72)*

**Full description:**

Your contacts ring as usual. When an unknown number calls, Gemma, your digital assistant, answers for you: she tells the caller right away that an AI is speaking, asks what it is about and then sends you a short summary with transcript and a call-back button.

How it works
• The app recognises calls from numbers that are not in your contacts and rejects them.
• Your "forward when busy" setting sends the call to your landline or SIP number.
• Gemma is registered there and has a short conversation (60 seconds max).
• You get the summary by push or email and see everything in the dashboard.

What you need
• An Android phone
• A FRITZ!Box with a landline number or a SIP provider (German-speaking service, German phone numbers)
• Your own Google Gemini API key (Google bills the call costs directly to you)
• A free gemivo account

Honestly
• Gemma takes messages. She does not book appointments, give out information or put calls through.
• The AI can mishear names; transcripts are generated automatically.
• "Forward when busy" also applies while you are on another call – then a contact can reach Gemma too.
• Costs: the app is free during the beta. Gemini usage is billed by Google via your key; call forwarding is charged by your mobile plan like a call to a landline.

Privacy: transcripts and summaries are stored on the gemivo server in Germany until you delete them. Audio is not recorded. Call audio is processed by Google (Gemini) via your own key. Your contacts are only checked on the device and are never uploaded.
